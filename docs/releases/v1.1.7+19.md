# Dart Tournament Manager 1.1.7+19

## Group draws and standings

- Applies the draw-wheel order consistently when building group assignments,
  matching the preview order in the groups and position editor.
- Uses direct head-to-head results before aggregate leg difference by default,
  updates saved tournaments that still use the previous default order, and
  applies the configured head-to-head criterion in advancement forecasts.
- Remembers the configured order locally on the device for future tournaments
  and explains match points, leg difference, legs won, and head-to-head criteria
  from the setup tooltips.

## Tournament duration

- Labels the recorded time as the actual tournament duration.
- Shows the estimated duration as the midpoint of the estimated range, followed
  by its minimum and maximum in parentheses, in results and the PNG export.
- Removes the average minutes-per-match duration label.
