# Dart Tournament Manager 2.7.5+95

- Avoids repeatedly scanning past tournament and monthly history while a new
  result is entered. Historical achievement unlocks are refreshed when a
  result is corrected or ranking inputs are explicitly changed.
- Adds profile-mode timeline spans around saving and achievement calculations
  for continued performance diagnosis.

The first achievement refresh after upgrading may perform a one-time historical
unlock scan to initialize the cache. This release's performance effect should
be confirmed with a new profile-mode trace on the same tournament and device.
